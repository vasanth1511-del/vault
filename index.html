Our purpose

Making studying feel less scattered.

StudyVault turns the mess of group chats and random links into a simple student-owned library.

01
⚠

The problem

Important notes and question papers are often lost in WhatsApp messages, old Drive links, and disconnected folders. Finding the right material takes time students do not have.

02
✦

The solution

StudyVault organizes student-shared resources by department, semester, subject, and type, so useful material is searchable when exams are near.

03
♧

The community

Students can add helpful links, upvote the best resources, and bookmark what they want to revisit. Small contributions make exam preparation easier for everyone.
